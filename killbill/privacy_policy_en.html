2. Overview

The App can be used without an account and is funded by advertising. Watching optional video ads (“rewarded ads”) unlocks rewards inside the App. We use advertising providers that process data about your device. Personalised advertising and tracking only happen with your consent.

3. Data we process

  • Device and advertising identifiers: Apple’s advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking, and the app-specific identifier (IDFV).
  • Technical data: IP address, device model, operating system version, language setting, approximate location (derived from the IP address), network connection.
  • Usage and ad data: ad views and interactions (for example videos watched, clicks), time an ad was shown.
  • In-app identifier: a randomly generated user ID stored on your device, used to assign rewards. It contains no information such as your name or email address.
  • Consent data: your choices in the consent dialog (TCF string) and your tracking decision.

The App itself does not collect names, email addresses, contacts, photos or health data.

4. Purposes and legal bases

  • Showing ads, including personalised ads, and measuring ads: based on your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR; Section 25(1) TDDDG for accessing information on your device).
  • Non-personalised ads, fraud prevention and security: based on our legitimate interest in funding and securely operating the App (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR), where consent is not required.
  • Granting rewards: to provide the feature you use (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Storing your consent decision: to meet our legal record-keeping obligations (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR).

5. Consent and App Tracking Transparency

On first launch, a consent dialog (Google User Messaging Platform, certified under the IAB Transparency & Consent Framework) asks whether advertising partners may process data for personalised advertising. In addition, iOS asks you via App Tracking Transparency whether the App may track you across other companies’ apps and websites.

You can withdraw your consent at any time with effect for the future: in the App under “Settings → Privacy settings” and in iOS under “Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking”. Without consent you will still see ads, but they will not be personalised.
